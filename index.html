Our Services

Comprehensive Transport Solutions

We provide end-to-end support for transport operators, ensuring compliance, efficiency, and peace of mind.

External Transport Manager

Professional transport management services to maintain your operator's licence. We act as your nominated Transport Manager, ensuring full DVSA compliance.

  • O-Licence compliance management
  • Vehicle maintenance oversight
  • Driver hours monitoring

Driver CPC Training

Comprehensive Driver Certificate of Professional Competence training courses. Keep your drivers qualified and up-to-date with the latest regulations.

  • Periodic training modules
  • Flexible scheduling options
  • JAUPT approved courses

Public Inquiry Consultant

Expert representation and support for Traffic Commissioner Public Inquiries. We prepare your case and stand by you throughout the process.

  • Pre-inquiry preparation
  • Document compilation
  • Hearing representation

Operator Licence Auditing

Thorough audits of your operations to identify compliance gaps before the authorities do. Protect your licence with our proactive approach.

  • Full compliance review
  • Gap analysis reporting
  • Remediation planning
About Us

Your Partner in Transport Compliance

Eastside Transport Consultancy Ltd is a leading UK transport support company specialising in auxiliary services for land transport operators. With over 15 years of industry experience, we've helped hundreds of operators maintain their licences, train their drivers, and navigate complex regulatory challenges.

Our team of industry experts understands the pressures facing transport operators today. We provide practical, cost-effective solutions that keep your business moving forward while ensuring full compliance with DVSA and Traffic Commissioner requirements.

Each 7-hour module contributes to the mandatory training hours.
